A better next move.
Before you apply.

Start with your ambition. See the tradeoffs. Build evidence. Compare the real-life value of an offer. Give employers a way to review work against a clear outcome.

This is an interactive product prototype.
The five Studio tools below use fictional examples and browser-local records. The free CV builder exports real PDFs; company profiles and free job posts now support account-owned cloud drafts and publishing. Job applications go directly to an employer-provided website or email. Live AI, employer verification, messaging, payments and real job applications are not connected.

Build your next chapter

Free CV builder: three templates, live preview, automatic PDF pagination and draft backup. Company profiles: brand, story, culture, benefits, team, locations and open roles. Save private drafts to your account and publish when ready.

What could make this distinctive

  1. Candidate-controlled opportunity briefs. Confidential ambitions and practical needs, with introductions only after mutual consent.
  2. Evidence with provenance. Reusable work with task context, ownership and transparent human review. Self-submitted and verified are different states.
  3. Paid, scoped work trials. A future marketplace with agreed compensation, ownership and dispute handling; today's challenges are fictional practice.
  4. Real-life offer value. Understand salary alongside time, commuting and recurring work costs.
  5. Visible hiring commitments. Actual review dates and outcome updates. Any reply rate must use recorded events, sample size and recency.
  6. Access beyond office careers. Practical workflows for household work, skilled roles and career transitions, with accessible language and clear duties.

The order that makes it real

1. Build the foundation: extend company-profile authentication, authorization and database storage to vacancies and applications; add private files, moderation and accessible Urdu/English flows.

2. Run a focused pilot: genuine employers and vacancies in Islamabad/Rawalpindi. Measure useful conversations, placements and retention.

3. Connect evidence and introductions: opt-in briefs, mutual consent and human-reviewed work.

4. Add intelligence and scale: evaluate AI assistance and expand where real employer supply and support exist. Show coverage and freshness for market insights.

Research context

Research references establish existing patterns, not a claim of exclusive novelty. See the original benchmark analysis, LinkedIn Hiring Assistant, Welcome to the Jungle's matching explanation and TestGorilla assessments.

A lasting advantage will come from trusted employer supply and better hiring outcomes. A larger feature list alone cannot create that.
